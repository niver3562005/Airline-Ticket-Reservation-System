Demonstration notice

AeroVista is an educational SE2030 project, not a commercial airline. Payment, email and SMS delivery are simulated and should never receive real card or passport details.

01

Information we collect

Booking records may include contact details, passenger names, date of birth, nationality, a demonstration passport reference, seat and cabin selection, meal preference, assistance requests and payment status. Account profiles contain sign-in and preference data. Travel-service requests can contain pickup, accommodation, SIM, dining, baggage or airport-assistance details.

02

How information is used

Data supports flight search, reservations, check-in, security and boarding readiness, refund handling, cancellation recovery, notifications, live operational displays, customer feedback and connected travel services. Validation and audit fields help protect data integrity.

03

Partners and communications

When a passenger requests a hotel, transfer, agency, café, SIM or airport priority service, the system records the selected partner or airport team and simulates a relevant email or SMS. No real third-party messages or payments are sent by this project.

04

Retention and security

Records remain in the configured project database for demonstration and assessment until an administrator removes the environment. Passwords are BCrypt-hashed, access is role-controlled, CSRF protection is enabled, and sensitive actions are validated on the server.

05

Your choices

Use profile settings to update contact preferences, the privacy centre to manage optional browser storage, and the support centre to request access, correction or deletion in a real deployment. This local demonstration does not perform automated advertising profiling.

Privacy questions: use the 24/7 support centre.
