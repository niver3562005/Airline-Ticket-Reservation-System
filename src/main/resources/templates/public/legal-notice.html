Built for learning. Designed like the real world.

Important limitations and conditions for the AeroVista reservation platform.

NoticeProject statusPaymentsTravel informationBrand & content
01

Educational project

AeroVista is a fictional airline reservation and operations system created for software-engineering assessment. It does not sell real tickets, operate flights or provide emergency services.

02

Payments and refunds

Card approval, bank transfer, refund references, email and SMS delivery are simulated. Never enter genuine payment credentials. Displayed fares and refund amounts have no monetary value.

03

Travel, visa and live-flight information

Visa rules, flight location, altitude, speed, airport facilities and service availability are demonstration data. Travellers must confirm real requirements with governments, airports and operating airlines.

04

Brand and content

AeroVista names, schedules and operational records are fictional. Third-party service descriptions are sample marketplace content. The interface is an original educational implementation inspired by modern premium travel experiences.
