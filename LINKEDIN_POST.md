Introducing **AeroVista** — a modern airline reservation and operations platform built around the complete passenger journey.

- Search and book flights with clear fares, cabin options and baggage allowances
- Manage passenger accounts, check-in, live status updates and travel notifications
- Reserve hotels, airport transfers, travel services and holiday packages
- Review cancellations and expected refunds before confirming
- Use dedicated workspaces for customers, airline staff and administrators

Built independently with Spring Boot, Thymeleaf, MVC, Spring Security, JPA and MySQL.
